    CQO Outcome Score Calculation & Interpretation Methodology

    Mathematical formulation, patient risk grounding, and clinical decision-making framework

    📐 Governing Mathematical Formulation
    Outcome Score = 7.60 + 0.45 · (ASAmean - 2.20) - 0.50 · (Outlier_Rate - 0.40) - 0.45 · (CPC / CPCmedian - 1.0)
    Bounded within: [5.20, 9.80]  |  Institutional Quality Target Baseline: 7.20 / 10.00

    1. Client Dataset Grounding & Parameter Derivation

    🩺 Patient Acuity Credit
    +0.45 × (ASAmean - 2.20)

    Derived from column asa_rating (ASA 1 to 4). Surgeons operating on complex, high-acuity systemic disease cohorts (ASA 3 & 4) receive an acuity credit so they are not penalized for complex case mix.

    ⚠️ Supply Anomaly Penalty
    -0.50 × (Outlier Rate - 0.40)

    Derived from Is_Master_Outlier and IF_Anomaly_Score (Isolation Forest). Penalizes erratic intraoperative supply churn, ad-hoc opened items, and excess kit waste.

    💰 Cost Stewardship Ratio
    -0.45 × (Cost Ratio - 1.0)

    Calculated as avg_cpc / median_cpc for the procedure. Assesses resource stewardship and financial discipline against peer surgeons for identical surgical indications.

    2. Clinical Value Quadrants & Interpretation Framework

    🟢 Clinical Champions (Top-Left)
    Low Cost (≤ Benchmark) & High Quality (Score ≥ 7.2)

    Surgeons delivering exceptional clinical outcomes with low supply waste. Action: Adopt their preference cards as the institutional standard BOM across the surgical department.

    🔵 Preference-Heavy (Top-Right)
    High Cost (> Benchmark) & High Quality (Score ≥ 7.2)

    High-quality outcomes, but driving elevated spend due to premium vendor brands or unrationalized custom packs. Action: Engage via Surgeon Bee for verified commodity brand substitutions.

    🟡 Under-Resourced (Bottom-Left)
    Low Cost (≤ Benchmark) & Quality Review (Score < 7.2)

    Low spend but lagging outcome metrics or high acuity without adequate instrumentation. Action: Review surgical pack completeness to ensure all required safety supplies are present.

    🔴 Urgent Alignment (Bottom-Right)
    High Cost (> Benchmark) & Quality Review (Score < 7.2)

    Significantly elevated Cost-Per-Case combined with high anomaly rates. Action: Priority clinical audit by Chief Medical Officer with mandatory lock-in to standardized preference card BOM.
